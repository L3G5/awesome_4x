Recommended policy: explicit trade consent

  1. Reuse the existing funded-offer flow for AI→player trade. Reserve the initiator’s30-credit treaty fee and spend its normal proposal order. Pending trade grants no income, border access or public treaty announcement. Acceptance spends the responder’s usual diplomatic order and consumes the reserved fee; it must not transfer that fee to the player or charge another30 credits. Decline/expiry refunds the proposer once. Keep existing player→AI and AI→AI immediate negotiation behavior for this bounded change.
  2. Make proposal status the primary UI fact. With a valid incoming peace offer, show “Their offer awaits your decision · expires cycle 31” and Review terms. Suppress the hypothetical new-proposal willingness sentence. Without an offer, show the actual action denial reason first (orders, credits, five-cycle minimum), then contextual willingness. After the minimum period, use “They are not yet willing to accept these terms” rather than implying negotiations are still barred.
  3. Preserve v1 commitments and keep offers specific. Treat legacy offers with no kind as peace; new offers may carry kind:'trade'. Validate and match kind, sender, recipient and ID before any debit or response. Legacy active treaties remain active. A pending trade offer must not be accepted as peace. Public treaty events happen only upon acceptance; war, elimination, expiry or an already concluded treaty invalidates/refunds pending trade. Use one filtered active-offer helper for badges, cards and dialogs so expired imported records cannot masquerade as live choices.

For AI behavior, retain its current willingness thresholds, treasury reserve checks and trade eligibility. A pending offer must prevent duplicate deposits; a proposal cooldown consistent with the existing peace cadence prevents repeated trade prompts after decline/expiry and after a completed cancellation. Existing peace offers remain valid despite later changes in willingness. Binding trade offers should likewise not be silently re-evaluated against a different trust score while still valid.

Acceptance checks for a future patch

Before consent, relations, border entry, income and public dispatches stay unchanged. Accept/decline/expiry alter escrow exactly once; response at zero orders is disabled without mutating anything. Wrong-kind, foreign-recipient, duplicate and stale responses fail atomically. The verified cycle 29 checkpoint must show an actionable pending settlement with no contradictory refusal. Legacy v1 peace offers and active trades must reload unchanged.
