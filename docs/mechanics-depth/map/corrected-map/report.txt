Assessment
This addresses the demonstrated initial access-starvation/outlier concern with little density pressure. It is preferable to forcing equal types or four identical opening stories. It does not prove balanced reward value or prevent economic snowballing: travel through hostile/closed space, a staged site's settlement cost, and the timing of grants still matter. It also does not establish that players enjoy the new exploration cadence.

The bounds apply at new generation on the initial active graph. Existing saves and player-opened lanes must not cause sites to relocate or constraints to be reapplied. A paid shortcut may intentionally bring a cluster into reach. This audit did not open lanes or certify that lifecycle; the separate lane tests/review must do so.
