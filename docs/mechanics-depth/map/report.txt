Independent map-generation audit: frozen map/peace prototype
Candidate a7c955d (full pin and engine hashes in source-manifest.json); baseline 810194596a0fa7d02efa418749bb3e27f547229e.
Fixed sample: ASTERION, then stage30-map-1 through stage30-map-511.

The new generator resamples actual space and satisfies the declared start/travel envelope in all 512 sampled maps. It does not establish better replayability or enjoyment. One substantive remaining issue is discovery access: some empires have no nearby discovery while another can reach several very early. The resource-pair guarantee does not resolve that difference.

Method and boundaries
scripts/map-generation-audit.mjs reconstructs each engine's import graph from pinned Git contents into a temporary directory. The supplied baseline archive was independently checked against8101945. Temporary source copies are removed; none are bundled. The candidate is frozen even while shared development continues with dormant lanes.

The script computes its own adjacency, BFS distances, bridges, articulation points and coordinate comparisons from serialized game states. It does not use the generator's describeGalaxyLayout to assert its metrics. It calls the generator only for its attempt/fallback diagnostics and verifies that its returned lanes match the actual campaign. Each candidate seed was generated twice to check identical full state. All saves validate. No end turns or campaign simulations are part of this512-seed measurement.

The fixed first32 seeds also exercise ordinary founding for all four factions:128 controlled command drills, each paying30 credits,20 minerals and two orders, consuming one Ark, setting population1 and leaving RNG unchanged. These are engine drills, not UI play. Sixteen separate initial-knowledge checks mutate hidden native values and foreign fleets, test unsurveyed discovery projections, and guard unsurveyed native reads during isolated initial research selection. Their scope is explicitly initial knowledge/selection, not every possible AI policy.

Results
- All 512 candidate maps pass the inspected count/connectivity, duplicate-edge, home degree, degree cap, separation, capital travel, diameter, local knowledge, safe-first-colony, recovery, resource tradeoff, resource-spread and discovery-deck checks. No fallback occurred.
- Attempts:271 maps pass on attempt1,117 on2,56 on3,36 on4,16 on5,9 on6,6 on7 and1 on8. Worst retry is stage30-map-7. The sample does not exercise the actual fallback branch or prove behavior over the full seed space.
- Candidate edges52-64 (median57), diameter10-18 (median13), all six capital-pair travel values5-13 (median8). Baseline edges51-62 (median56), diameter10-27 (median15), capital travel5-23 (median7). The change removes a long isolation tail; it does not merely shorten every journey.
- Minimum world separation is >=6.30079 in the candidate. The baseline sample can place worlds0.1 units apart. This is a concrete geometry/legibility improvement, not a measurement of players' map-reading performance.
- Neutral positions move by mean46.60 units when compared by world ID, but that includes ID reassignment. The ID-independent symmetric nearest-point distance between paired old/new constellations averages6.78 units (range5.79-8.01), supporting real spatial resampling.
- Concord has467 distinct labeled neighbor sets versus7 in the baseline. Do not interpret this alone as strategic variety: shuffling neutral IDs contributes. Both old and new maps already have512 distinct capital-colored graph refinement signatures. Signature uniqueness is not a fun metric or even a proof of isomorphism when two signatures match.
- The candidate has504 distinct six-capital-distance vectors. Bridges range0-16, median2 versus baseline3; articulation points median4 versus6.144 candidate maps have no bridge. The mixture retains some long arms and junctions, but somewhat more looped access could reduce the value of choke defense. It should be observed in play, not automatically counted as deeper strategy.
- All 2048 faction starts survey exactly capital+two adjacent prospects, with4-10 charted systems (median6). The baseline surveys only the capital and charts3-6 (median4). Knowledge does not consume extra scouting orders.
- Across these starts,1048 have one ordinary and one Ecology-dependent prospect;1000 have two ordinary prospects. Ore versus science advantages remain opposed, and native ore+science pair totals range8-14 with at most4 difference between empires in a map. There are always at least two ordinarily habitable unowned worlds within three jumps, including a non-prospect recovery world in the empire's closer catchment. Median ordinary worlds within three jumps is5 in both candidate and baseline.
- The pair's raw total is not an economic fairness score. A high-ore world behind Ecology and a readily settleable high-ore world have different timing costs. Nor does a known alternative mean two equally compelling first-settlement choices:1048 of the pairs have only one immediately eligible prospect.
- Discovery site sets vary in all 512 candidate maps and cover all38 neutral world identities. The baseline uses the same ten identities in every map. Candidate content keeps one of every ordinary type and exactly two duplicated ordinary types, plus one Observatory and one Reef. No initial prospect contains a discovery. This removes fixed named reward targets and bounds repeated event types; its effects on interest remain unmeasured.

Concrete remaining critique: discovery-access starvation
The new deck distributes type repetition but site selection is unconstrained by reachability. Nearest discovery across faction starts ranges1-8 jumps.118/2048 starts have none within three jumps;29 have none within four. One start may have five sites within two jumps. Immediate portable sites are capped at two by the observed local geometry/excluded prospect arrangement, but early two-hop clusters remain possible.

Most pronounced sampled case: stage30-map-458. Choir's nearest discovery is8 jumps away, versus Khar1, Veyr2 and Concord3. Choir has only three ordinarily habitable worlds within3 jumps. This is not an invalid survival start, but a large difference in access to exploration content and early portable science/mineral/ship opportunities. A two-jump scout needs at least four outward travel cycles just to reach that first site before any detour, border or order constraint. The sample alone does not prove the reward disparity decides a race.

Cluster counterexample: stage30-map-22. Choir has five sites within2 jumps (three ordinary and both staged sites), Concord four, while Veyr and Khar have none within2 and first discoveries at3. Staged restoration requires investment and ownership, so counting those sites as free power would overstate the disparity. They nevertheless offer more early commitments and information than an eight-jump void.

Recommended correction: guarantee a minimum nearby discovery OPPORTUNITY per empire, preserving ten sites, the existing shuffled type deck and no fixed reward/type. A distance floor such as at least one site within3 ordinary jumps is a testable first bound; assign only its location, then shuffle types. Use strict-nearest home catchments when necessary to avoid counting one contested site as every empire's safety promise. Keep the remaining sites variable. Do not give four identical event openings or compensate with guaranteed science jackpots. Rerun the SAME512 seeds against the corrected candidate in a separate output directory, retaining this a7c955d result unchanged.

Other repetition and untested questions
- Every capital still receives a three-prong inward local catchment, and every opening presents a deliberately authored ore/science pair. That is a useful legible scaffold, but it can become a new recognizable routine. External routes, climate, discovery competition and the second settlement must make its timing/context matter.
- Neutral native rolls, placement, safety edits, starting knowledge and discovery distribution all changed. This is a generator bundle, not a causal experiment isolating coordinates. Do not attribute future policy wins to geometry alone.
- Bounds deliberately suppress severe isolation and dense overlaps. Whether the remaining range feels sufficiently different, or overly standardized, requires repeated openings and late defense observation.
- A minimum nearby discovery is not equal reward value or a representative fairness study. The worst ordinary settlement start, strongest early find, and recovery after rivals contest a route still need play.
- The engine stores a full world state in saves; these checks concern gameplay projections and decisions, not an anti-cheat security boundary. In particular getVisibleSystems returns internal system objects, so callers must continue to gate native information by survey and ships by sensors.

  node scripts/map-generation-audit.mjs --candidate CORRECTED_COMMIT --out docs/mechanics-depth/map/corrected

The compressed data is geometry/knowledge/controlled-drill evidence, not engagement evidence.
