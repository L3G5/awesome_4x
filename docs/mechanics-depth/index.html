Changes and tradeoffs

  • Geography changes between seeds. New42-system maps vary positions, connections, surveyed ore/science prospects and discovery placement. The first512-map audit exposed a start eight jumps from any discovery. The corrected generator guarantees a site within three original-graph jumps and limits nearby concentration, while keeping types and rewards variable. Existing saves retain their original geography.
  • Dormant starlanes are shared investments. Logistics and an owned endpoint permit a normal paid construction project:60credits,70minerals,48work and one order. The finished edge uses ordinary border, movement and combat rules for every empire. Queued courses remain unchanged until deliberately replotted. Cancellation and capture follow queue rules.
  • Show the route that matters. Restoration previews compare charted access to your capital and other colonies, including unchanged routes and unreachable destinations. A queued job shows its actual builder and remaining estimate. Rival production and hidden fleet locations remain private. Ordinary Empire commissioning retains the comparison, scroll and keyboard result.
  • Blockade-aware peace. A government whose own fleet blocks a rival’s public Beacon weighs whether the five-cycle truce could let that rival finish alignment. Losses, pressure and weariness still matter; already-funded offers keep their binding terms. The review also corrected an existing Russian translation failure in ordinary peace terms.
  • Undo actions within the cycle. Full prior-state snapshots restore spending, orders, movement, discoveries, RNG, consumed fleets, reports and diplomacy escrow. Free research/course/Hold changes also count; failed commands and no-ops do not. End Cycle commits the turn, even when a rival wins before the counter increments. Confirmed import and new campaign clear history. Browser history survives reload only when it matches the exact saved state; autosave takes priority if storage is full.
  • Mobile inspection is deliberate. A new or restored narrow game starts with no active fleet. Tapping a world inspects it, leaving a52px roster control. Selecting a fleet enables destination previews; Close returns to inspection without altering its course or Hold. Review corrected a landscape overlap, notifications covering Undo, a narrow SPACE badge and offscreen focus after modal Undo.
