Design hypotheses

  1. Geography: new campaigns should present different local resource, settlement and access problems. Vary actual positions and connections, retain a connected42-system galaxy and four viable starts, and distribute discoveries independently of fixed system IDs. Imported saves retain their geography. Acceptance requires plausible plan reversals, not merely different names.
  2. Dormant starlanes: restoration is a paid construction commitment at an owned endpoint. A completed edge benefits every empire under ordinary movement, order and border rules. Show the builder’s time/cost, known travel savings and shared-use risk before commitment. Preserve existing plotted courses until the player deliberately replots.
  3. Peace and public victory: an empire maintaining a blockade should weigh the risk that a proposed truce lets its opponent complete public alignment. Losses, pressure and eventual weariness remain counterweights; already-funded offers retain their terms. Explanations must use information both parties can know.
  4. Execution: commission colony work from Empire comparison without losing the selected world, scroll or keyboard focus. Keep English and Russian interface coverage and existing export/import behavior.

Evidence required before completion

  • Controlled map generation checks across fixed seeds, including worst starts, actual connectivity and strategic route diversity.
  • Ordinary paid restoration through construction completion, legal movement on the new edge, rival participation, cancellation/capture/reload and visibility boundaries.
  • Contrasting decisions where restoring a lane is useful and where delaying or avoiding it is justified by opportunity cost or exposure.
  • Focused peace scenarios with and without an active blockade, imminent public alignment, military pressure and existing funded offers.
  • Independent critique and real UI play of the integrated mechanics, followed by corrections supported by those observations.
  • Relevant rules/browser regressions, English/Russian checks, a successful build, deployment and hosted verification.
  • At least four elapsed hours of substantive development, evaluation and iteration; timing alone is not completion.

This page records the intended scope before implementation. Results and rejected approaches will be labeled as they become available. No claim of measured human enjoyment or representative win rates is implied.

Prototype findings

The first512-map audit exposed discovery-access outliers that geometry bounds alone missed. The corrected generator preserves positions, active routes and local prospects while ensuring a discovery within three initial jumps and limiting sites within two jumps to three. Discovery types and rewards remain variable.

Paid shared lanes are integrated with ordinary queues, movement, visibility and save validation. The AI accounts for construction delay, already-paid traffic, reserves, public finish horizons and visible hostile use. A32-seed passive-player cohort produced two funded completions and two observed crossings; it is integration evidence, not a measure of enjoyment or balance.

Independent interface review found an ambiguous hypothetical duration when viewing an existing project from the other endpoint. The next candidate shows only the actual project’s builder and remaining estimate. Initial browser-driver assertions about sort direction and Russian capitalization were corrected separately from product behavior.
