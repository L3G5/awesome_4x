Findings (observed, high confidence)

1. Medium — an active restoration mixes the actual project with an unpayable new quote. Start at Eos (25 production,2 cycles), then open restoration from idle Nacre (7 production). The panel presents “PAY NOW”60 credits/70 minerals/1 order and a7cycle construction estimate, followed by the true Eos2 cycle project. Actual state and paid effect are correct; the display can mislead a player comparing completion time. Evidence: preview-en-1440-underway-from-other and preview-ru-390-underway-from-other text/screens. Recommendation: while underway, show only actual builder/remaining time and an Inspect construction link; do not quote an alternate project. Counterexample: an unstarted lane must retain both costs and builder choice because that is a real choice.

2. Medium — direct inspector commits lose the result context after a builder change. Inspect Nacre, Review restoration, choose Eos, focus Restore and press Enter. Eos receives the correct queue and the unrelated held scout stays selected, but the inspector remains Nacre and focus falls to BODY. Reproduced all 4language/width cases. Evidence: lifecycle-results.json directAlternateBuilderFocus and controlled-lifecycle-*-direct-alternate-committed screenshots. Recommendation: on a Galaxy-origin commit, inspect the actual builder and focus its new production row while retaining fleet selection/intent. Counterexample: an Empire-origin commit should remain Empire and focus the chosen colony row, which already works.

3. Low/medium — opening the enabled restoration directly focuses Restore and scrolls the narrow dialog past its title (title y=-11px English/-36px Russian). This starts reading midway through the decision. Evidence: controlled-lifecycle-en-390-direct-enabled and controlled-lifecycle-ru-390-direct-enabled screenshots/metrics. Recommendation: focus Close and open at the beginning; keep actionable costs and travel before expandable procedural detail. Counterexample: opening a tab or replacing a selected builder should preserve the relevant control focus rather than always resetting to Close.

Test limitations and corrections

- controlled-base.json is deliberately synthetic: both endpoints plus 4 extra colonies belong to the player, all systems are charted/surveyed, resources/technology are supplied, rival actors are inactive, and a test frigate starts at the endpoint. These fixtures isolate lifecycle/UI behavior; they establish neither balance nor rival pressure.
- Ordinary cases use the unmodified actual default campaign cycle 50 save. English1440/390 and Russian1440 preserve exact scroll on commit. Russian390 preserves the selected row, fleet, state, export/reload and horizontal 180px, but shifts vertical 383→388px; this minor anchoring drift is explicitly recorded rather than called exact preservation.
- lifecycle-results.json preserves the first attempts. Four crossing cases stopped on an ambiguous test selector (the destination appears in both lane and ordinary-neighbor lists); lifecycle-corrected.mjs selects the lane destination explicitly and passes all 4 complete cases. The original strict 5px scroll failure is retained; ordinary-scroll-observation.mjs checks the bounded drift and completes state/export/reload checks. No product state failure was concealed by those driver corrections.
- This is a focused usability/lifecycle review, not a completed campaign or a population estimate of player enjoyment. Natural play and private-information checks are recorded against the later0.23.0 candidate.
