Asterion Reach — conservative AI restoration policy
Review basis: core/UI commit a5077ea9fd3fca2cf5be834bc1715ad1c951135e plus uncommitted policy in src/ai.js and new tests/starlane-ai.test.js. No staging or commits by this agent. Root's existing completedInvestment starlane guard is preserved.

Behavior
- getStarlanePlan(state, factionId) is exported and pure. It neither reserves resources nor changes RNG, orders, queues, knowledge, strategy, or save metadata.
- Concrete jobs come from existing Colony Arks, already paid queued Arks/military hulls, and current ordinary military objective/rally/Beacon-defense routes. Colony target selection is shared with civilian execution; military routing uses the same weighted route() implementation with an optional hypothetical edge. Unpaid future traffic earns no credit.
- This cycle's movement precedes economics. An existing fleet is projected along its current ordinary route for T−1 further movement phases before T-cycle construction completes. Paid hulls start from their normal queue release bound. Ordinary missions use one jump per phase; the same public-race selector in a pure refreshed-movement view identifies a selected expedition's normal two jumps. Growth can release a queued hull earlier than the displayed current-production ETA, so an optimistic population bound prevents a late road from being credited with a hull already past its junction.
- A counted job must still save at least two remaining jumps and actually traverse the proposed edge. Total saved actions must be at least three. Its conservative arrival must precede the campaign limit, any active public alignment horizon, and the owner's currently ready/resuming alignment horizon. Publicly announced trade expiry can invalidate the route.
- Normal price remains 60 credits, 70 minerals, 48 work, one order. Existing Beacon/payment reserves survive. Existing/paid Ark founding budgets retain 30 credits and 20 minerals each; at least 45 credits of working capital remain. Credit income must cover paid future crews plus the ordinary four-credit floor, or a 60-credit alignment floor.
- Any currently observed home threat vetoes optional work. For shared use, only currently observed hostile military hulls are tested on the builder's chart. A candidate is rejected if it shortens a hostile approach by two or more jumps to an inadequately defended own colony, leaving that colony within three charted jumps. This uses visible hull type/count, not foreign weapon research, rival private charts, hidden fleets, orders, or routes.
- Economics now funds emergency readiness, Beacons, income, necessary hulls and Ark priorities throughout the empire before considering one restoration; discretionary charters and complements follow. The command is ordinary restoreLane. New edges are then used naturally by existing routing. No extra fleet movement, work, resources, sensors or saved route rewrites are granted.

Limits and tradeoffs
The planner deliberately misses some profitable infrastructure. It assumes fast ordinary departure during construction, does not forecast future combat delays or beneficial diplomatic changes, does not value speculative future production traffic, and uses conservative public finish horizons even if a race might later pause. A shared-use risk estimate permits hostile transit through the builder's chart without asking about unknown third-party border permissions, so it can overestimate danger. Peaceful rivals' possible future use remains a general strategic cost, not a predicted hostile plan. Queue commitments continue normally if objectives later change. The 2/32 funding rate demonstrates reachable behavior, not a tuned ideal frequency or stronger AI. No new displayed English strings or localization keys were introduced.
