A race worth responding to

40 of sixty rival empires begin alignment in the passive cohort. Their 6 interruptions demonstrate that reaching the network does not always produce ten uninterrupted advancing cycles. Interruptions count active-to-inactive transitions. This metric alone cannot distinguish a blockade, lost beacon ownership, and a funding shortfall.

War exposes the policy's limits

The military controller can open several wars while continuing to commission ships. It wins 3 of twenty campaigns. Its frequent defeats reveal the cost of this controller's aggression; they do not establish that military play is uncompetitive.

03

What each controller actually does

Every controller keeps the Concord's ordinary traits and pays the shared command API's real prices. No resources are granted, and targeting uses public knowledge plus currently visible fleets. Three active policies add their priorities before the shared AI carries out its remaining turn.

Passive observer

The player issues no commands. Measures autonomous rival development and race completion.

Science policy

Prioritizes the Protocol research path, commissions available laboratories and Academy Charters, then uses the shared AI for exploration, expansion, fleet control and remaining development.

Research priorities

physics → logistics → computing → industry → singularity → ecology → diplomacy → shields → command → weapons → cruisers → terraforming

Military policy

Prioritizes military research and affordable warships. At 75 military power, may declare war on a contacted empire if own power exceeds 1.4 times visible enemy power. Does not cap simultaneous wars. Uses shared AI for remaining actions.

Research priorities

weapons → logistics → shields → cruisers → command → ecology → diplomacy → industry → physics → computing → singularity → terraforming

Balanced policy

Uses a mixed research order and the shared AI for exploration, expansion, military control, economic specialization and beacon commitments.

Research priorities

logistics → ecology → physics → industry → diplomacy → weapons → computing → shields → singularity → command → cruisers → terraforming

Timing is conditional on reaching a milestone. A campaign can end before an empire completes all research or starts alignment. Counts remain visible beside every median so those unfinished paths are not silently removed from the interpretation. First-war timing describes any war in the galaxy, not necessarily a war involving the player.

Measurement rules

  • Run the same twenty seeds for each policy through a winner or cycle 120.
  • Record technology, charter, beacon and alignment milestones after completed cycles.
  • Validate save structure and nonnegative credits, minerals and orders at every sampled cycle.
  • Report conventional medians; for an even sample, average the middle pair.
  • Keep the existing faction differences. These measurements do not isolate every individual rule change.
  • Use separate focused regressions for emergency reserves, hidden information, blockade recovery and simultaneous finishes.
