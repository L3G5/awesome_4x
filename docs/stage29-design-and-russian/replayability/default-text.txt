One campaign, two interface versions
The
original opening
used ordinary visible controls on version 0.21.0 and reached cycle 12. Its untouched export was imported through the normal file picker into immutable version 0.21.1, then played to the natural ending. There were no save edits, grants, strategy automation, engine commands or rollback.
The final replay begins at that actual cycle-12 export; it does not reconstruct cycles 1–12. The fourteen engine modules are identical across the played continuation and released version 0.21.2, as recorded in the
source comparison
. This establishes engine continuity, not a claim that the older interface was the current release.
The decisions and their consequences
Four founding worlds were identified; Nacre became the fifth colony at cycle 34. Academies, a Forge and ordinary fleet reinforcement supported a compact empire.
Three Beacons came online before the alignment run. Choir declared war at 54 and Khar at 55, ending two trade agreements while funding remained sufficient.
Halcyon lost a cruiser in its first defense. Paid repairs, a new cruiser and two Shipyard replacements rebuilt the defensive positions.
Haven’s four-frigate lead was destroyed across the final attack. The remaining cruiser defeated the last attacker and stood at 12.5/85 hull after passive repair. Alignment reached 10/10 without interruption.
The final empire retained four cruisers, one frigate and one scout, with Legacy 607 and 1,902 credits. Five combat reports and all 138 Chronicle entries remain in the final save. The
player report
links actual checkpoints, decisions, screenshots and the complete request ledger.
What was independently checked

Scope:

node docs/default-campaign/replay.mjs --out /tmp/asterion-default-reproduction
