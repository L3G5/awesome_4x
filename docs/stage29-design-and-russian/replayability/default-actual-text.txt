Decisions that shaped the campaign
Cycles 13–30 — colony development and survey.
Founded Halcyon at 13. Chose Academy I at Sundrift and Haven and Forge I at Solace; balanced their up-front cost with Enclaves, Manufactories, and Extractors. A scout surveyed the frontier while the initial frigate returned home. A newly commissioned frigate merged with it at 26, retaining Hold and the chosen defense lead. An Ark departed for Nacre before the cycle 30 checkpoint. All three trade/open-border agreements were still active.
Cycles 31–43 — fifth world and the Beacon investment.
Founded Nacre at 31 and completed the visible survey of all 42 systems by 33. A third frigate joined the home formation. Researched Asterion Protocol at 36, then commissioned Beacons at Sundrift (36), Haven (39), and Halcyon (43). Foreign Khar–Veyr war appeared in the briefing without involving the Concord.
Cycles 44–53 — preparing to protect alignment.
The first completed Beacon exposed the funding card, which correctly separated ample funds from the incomplete three-Beacon requirement. Researched cruiser technology and commissioned cruisers at the Forge. Distributed defenses across the three sites. A 35-credit gift to the Choir improved trust while visibly retaining existing grievances. The third Beacon completed at 51 and alignment began; it advanced every cycle through 60.
Cycles 54–57 — two wars and a real repair tradeoff.
The Choir declared war in the cycle 54 briefing and Khar in 55, breaking their trade agreements. Peace terms were initially unavailable. Moved reinforcements using the visible lanes and merged two cruisers at Halcyon. Replaced Extractors with Shipyards at Haven and Halcyon after reading the investment comparison: local minerals fell from 9 to 4, while the shared repair pool rose from 4 to 8 hull; each Beacon remained intact. Halcyon repelled five Choir frigates, losing one cruiser. Three ordinary paid repairs restored the survivor. A local engagement cleared a hostile scout at Solace, freeing the newly built cruiser to reinforce Halcyon.
Cycles 58–60 — holding the network.
Inspected peace terms again; no accepted settlement was available, and no invalid proposal was submitted. Merged a new frigate into Haven’s lead formation and stationed another at Sundrift. Khar attacked Haven during the final resolution. Its attackers were destroyed; four Concord frigates were lost, but the cruiser and Beacon survived. Alignment reached 10/10.
Actual cycle 30 checkpoint: four colonies, active development, and an Ark en route to the fifth world.
