Observed defects requiring catalog/pattern changes
High confidence; medium severity overall, higher for decision and accessibility text. Numbers and saved state remained intact in the tested commands.
1. System/type, charter and construction fragments leak English: Terran world; Academy Charter I/II; Forge Charter I; +6 science; +2 orbital repair; +10% military mineral saving; 2 cycles. These appear in both current decisions and recorded history.
2. Network and route fragments leak or miscompose: 2 / 3 beacons; 2 restored beacons; This world participates in Concord’s alignment.; / 10 cycles; route header Прыжков: · 3 · 3 orders total. The route title's leading-dot capture is also awkward Russian ordering.
3. Decision text in diplomacy remains mixed: Trade income +10 credits за цикл · Open borders · Proposals use 1 приказ; Military access · Hostile fleets engage on arrival · Proposals use 1 приказ. Faction mottos inside curly quotes remain English.
4. Peace card summary is entirely English: Receive 60 credits and agree to a 5-cycle mutual truce. Fleets inside each other's territory return to their nearest colony when accepted. The modal's withdrawal term is corrupted to Вывод военных сил. Осталось: Fleets inside each other's territory return to their nearest owned colony and lose movement. This is not just an untranslated proper name.
5. Battle UI leaks Engagement, attacker, defender and rounds. Historical summary translation captures the wrong fragment: Битва в системе Haven: «Кхар»: потеряно кораблей — 1, прочности — 35.9 hull; Concord lost 0 ships and 80.5. The original attacker/defender casualty values remain visible but their connective sentence is broken.
6. Chronicle war text captures the rest of the English treaty-betrayal sentence as if it were the target faction: «Доминион Кхар» объявляет войну державе «The Concord. The active trade treaty was broken; the betrayal deepens their grievance».
7. Resource title/tooltips stay English (Credits: 1847; +110 per cycle after 11 fleet upkeep, before alignment payments; Minerals; Science; Orders). Many galaxy accessible names remain fully English after the system name, including founding world/concord/beacon/route preview jump/destination. These are real presentation attributes, not save data.
8. Conclusion retained-count lines mix suffixes without spacing: Записей в Летописи: 138entries · cycles 1–60; Боевых отчётов: 5reports · cycles 55–59. Final beacon headings also leak Beacons.
9. Exploration history retains whole English outcomes for The Last Observatory, The Silent Archive and The Hollow Sun. Their details were reached through actual recorded Chronicle/conclusion text, not only source enumeration.
