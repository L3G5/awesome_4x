Remaining findings
All are high-confidence observations. Severity is medium overall; accessible-name failures directly affect Russian assistive-technology use. No wrong cost, treaty duration, withdrawal behavior or full-state mutation was observed in the tested dialogs. This does not establish that every unseen dynamic string is correct.

R1. Several previously translated operational fragments regressed to English.
• Map: “42 charted systems · 42 surveyed · код генерации ASTERION”. Phase 1 showed “Систем на карте: 42 · исследовано: 42 · код генерации: ASTERION”.
• Menu: “Cycle 50 · ASTERION · The Concord”. Phase 1 localized Cycle and Concord while preserving the seed.
• Funding: “Funding is covered.” precedes the Russian missing-beacon requirement. Phase 1 showed “Средств достаточно.”
• The replacement warning translates its first sentence, then retains “Its benefits are then removed. Cancelling construction keeps the existing infrastructure.” Both sentences were Russian in phase 1.
• Ship card costs end in “1 upkeep”, “2 upkeep” or “4 upkeep”; phase 1 showed “Содержание: …”.
• The right Chronicle context still shows “1 dispatches” / “8 dispatches”.
Evidence: text/default50-1440-{galaxy,menu,empire,construction,ships,journal}.txt and corresponding screenshots. Narrow copies reproduce the same fragments.

R2. Battle and other history clauses remain English; some research records regressed.
• Actual finished60's battle summary reads “Battle at Haven: Khar lost 1 ship and 35.9 hull; Concord lost 0 ships and 80.5 hull. Атакующий флот уничтожен.” The same English casualty clauses appear in the briefing, Chronicle and conclusion. The final candidate avoids the earlier wrong-name capture, but it does not translate this clause.
• Some research records now read “Research complete: Autonomous Industry. …”, “Research complete: Capital Ship Architecture. …” or “Research complete: Coherent Lances. …”. Other entries translate the completion prefix and technology but leave the entire English effect sentence. Their counterparts were fully Russian in phase 1.
• Actual expedition records still begin “The Last Observatory: Take the timing core.”, “The Silent Archive: Recover their knowledge.” and “The Hollow Sun: Redirect the foundries.” Resource grants now translate; the event/choice prefix does not.
• Conclusion count spacing and entry/report nouns are fixed, but the battle-report range still says “cycles 55–59”.
Evidence: text/finished60-1440-{battle-rounds,briefing,journal,conclusion}.txt; text/default50-1440-journal.txt; corresponding 390px captures. Names and numbers in the canonical save remain unchanged.

R3. Galaxy accessible names and a few tooltips are not fully localized.
• “Solace, founding world, concord” and “Haven, ✦ concord beacon, route preview jump 2 of 2, destination”. Other map labels retain arid/ocean/volcanic/unsurveyed/adjacent-to-selected-fleet descriptors.

Evidence: text/*-latin.json, attribute entries, and remaining-English.json. These are presentation attributes, not English canonical save data. Resource tooltips themselves are now translated correctly.

The complete bounded inventory is remaining-English.json: 32 distinct rendered mixed/English lines and 39 attribute variants. These are diagnostic counts, not a completion percentage.
