Interface and engine review · Historical campaign evidence

Trade consent across complete campaigns

All 24 campaigns passed: 1,689 ordinary End-cycle commands, 1,859 valid checked states, 29,744 finite nonnegative resource values and 75 exact next-round JSON reload comparisons. No player trade treaty opened without an explicit acceptance.

Fixed cohort

Eight matched seeds run under three otherwise-passive policies: ignore incoming trade, accept every legal proposal, or decline every legal proposal. The policies use ordinary commands and never move, develop colonies, research, fight, cancel treaties or answer peace offers. Each campaign ends at its first winner, with the existing cycle-120 bound. The unmodified engine is pinned to 123cee1a4e2b8175713468b2d8d9c3fc0d5787cf.

All 250 proposal creations respected previous quiet deadlines. The 1,215 observations within quiet intervals passed both forecast and AI-intent checks. Repeated checks retained funded terms; each resolved offer transitioned once, and no pending receipt disappeared. Declining starts the quiet interval earlier than waiting for expiry, so that policy receives more proposals over the same campaign duration.

Financial measurement

All 146 explicit responses checked actual treasury deltas and one-order cost. Accepting neither charged nor paid the recipient; declining refunded exactly 30 credits to the proposer. Receipt totals account for 7,500 reserved credits: 450 consumed fees, 6,870 refund obligations and 180 still reserved across six terminal pending offers.

Of those refund obligations, 3,930 credits were checked as isolated decline-command deltas; 2,940 are expiry obligations derived from resolved receipts. Income and AI spending share the End-cycle operation, so this campaign audit does not isolate expiry treasury deltas. Focused rule tests separately check exact expiry refunds. No post-victory refunds are invented for pending offers.

Coverage and limits

The campaigns exercised acceptance, repeated refusal, natural expiry and quiet deadlines. They observed no early invalidations, treaty closures or archive pruning; the largest saved offer archive held 24 records, below its 32-record pruning trigger. These paths are not claimed as campaign-tested here. The historical cancellation experiment and focused rules checks cover separate cases.

These deliberately passive policies do not measure human difficulty, win rates or improvement over an earlier version. Matching seeds do not guarantee matching outcomes after accepting trade. The 150 reload probe states are separate from the main-cohort state count and contribute no offers or cycles to its totals.
